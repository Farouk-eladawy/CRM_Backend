TourCare.ai

Service List

An AI operating system for tour and activity companies that sell on GetYourGuide, Viator, Headout, and Tiqets. It takes bookings, guest messages, pickup times, and reviews into one place, then handles the repetitive work.

TourCare is built for desert safari operators, city-tour companies, attraction providers, and inbound DMCs. After a booking arrives, the system can confirm the guest, collect the hotel and meeting point, send the pickup time, remind them before the tour, and follow up after the experience.

1. AI Operations System

The main service. It runs the tour from the moment a booking arrives until the experience is finished, and turns the daily manual tasks into automatic steps.

  • Booking inbox. New bookings from your platforms arrive in one list, without copying them by hand.
  • Instant confirmation. The guest receives an automatic reply after booking, with the main details confirmed.
  • Guest details collection. The assistant asks for the hotel name, room number, or map location by email or WhatsApp.
  • Pickup messages. Pickup time is sent by email or WhatsApp, with a reminder before the tour.
  • Extra payments and invoices. Optional extras can be collected through a payment link, and an invoice can go out with the welcome or pickup message.
  • Message templates. Ready texts for confirmation, reminder, pickup time, invoice, review request, and complaint follow-up, written in more than one language.
  • Last-minute alerts. A booking that comes in close to departure is sent to the operations manager on WhatsApp.
  • Team accounts. Guides, drivers, and suppliers can have their own access to update the status of a trip.
  • Daily report. An email each day with booking counts by platform, destination, and product.
  • Performance dashboard. Bookings, revenue, cancellations, ratings, and reviews in one view.

2. Smart Pickup & Tracking

The transport service. It organizes hotel pickups and shows where each driver and guest is, so tours leave on time and fewer guests are marked as no-shows.

  • Pickup time by location. The meeting time is calculated from the guest’s hotel or pin, then sent on WhatsApp, email, or SMS.
  • Live map. Driver and guest locations are visible while the pickup is happening.
  • Delay alerts. The team is notified if a driver is late or the guest’s location changes.
  • Operations board. Every pickup for the day is shown on one screen.
  • Private contact. The driver can reach the guest without either phone number being exposed.
  • Arrival photo. The driver can send a photo from the meeting point so the guest knows the vehicle is there.
  • Backup location. If the guest does not appear, a live location can be shared at the backup time to help them arrive and reduce refund requests.
  • Driver reports. Performance, no-shows, and cancellations are summarized so the team can see where pickups fail.

3. Live Quality Support

The after-tour service. It combines the assistant with a quality process to request reviews, catch unhappy guests early, and close complaints and refund requests.

  • 24/7 guest answers. Common questions are answered at any hour in the guest’s language. A person on your team can take over when the case needs a human.
  • Review requests. After the tour, a polite multilingual message asks the guest to leave a review.
  • Early warning. The system flags a guest who sounds unhappy before that guest writes a public review.
  • Complaint file. Details and reasons are collected so the quality team can follow the case and correct the service.
  • Guest recovery. The team can offer a clear alternative before the guest asks for a refund or posts a negative review.
  • Refund follow-up. Refund requests are tracked step by step until they are closed, with the conversation saved.
  • Review removal follow-up. Reviews that break a platform’s own rules can be raised with that platform for removal.
  • Monthly quality report. A short report explains why guests were satisfied or not, for the management team.

How a new company starts

  1. Connect. Your supplier account on GetYourGuide, Viator, Headout, or Tiqets is linked so bookings can be read.
  2. Configure. Pickup rules, message templates, and team access are set for your tours.
  3. Go live. Confirmations, pickup times, reminders, and review requests run on their own. Your team steps in only when a guest needs a person.

The first step is a free consultation and a live demonstration on your own type of tours. Nothing in this list is a price quote.

Platforms named above are the booking channels TourCare connects to. WhatsApp message fees charged by the phone provider, and card fees charged by a payment company, are separate from TourCare’s service.
