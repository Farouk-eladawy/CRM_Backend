FTS Travels (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates tours and customer-support communications, including a WhatsApp Business application connected to Meta platforms. This page is the public Privacy Policy for that application and for our related websites and customer services. It can be read without creating an account or signing in.

1. Who we are

FTS Travels is an Egyptian travel company providing excursions and related services in destinations such as Hurghada, Cairo, Luxor, Sharm El Sheikh, and Marsa Alam. Our WhatsApp Business app is used to message customers about bookings, pickup times, support, and operational updates.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you contact us, we may collect:

  • Name, phone number, email address, and hotel or pickup details
  • Booking numbers, trip dates, passenger counts, and travel preferences
  • WhatsApp and Facebook message content, media, delivery status, and phone numbers provided through Meta WhatsApp Cloud API or Messenger
  • Passport or ID details only when required for a booking
  • Technical data such as IP address, device type, and message timestamps

3. How we use information

  • To confirm, operate, and support bookings
  • To reply to customer and partner messages on WhatsApp, email, and related channels
  • To send operational notices such as pickup times, meeting points, and itinerary updates
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and service disruption
  • To comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations

We do not sell personal information. We do not use WhatsApp or Facebook data to build advertising profiles unrelated to our travel services.

4. Meta, WhatsApp, and Facebook data

When you message our business on WhatsApp or related Meta products, Meta provides us with the information needed to receive and send those messages (for example your phone number and message content). We process that data only to provide customer support and booking operations. Meta’s own processing is described in Meta’s privacy policy.

5. Sharing

We may share information with:

  • Authorized FTS Travels staff who handle bookings and customer support
  • Service providers such as hotels, drivers, guides, and payment processors, only as needed to deliver the trip
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. / WhatsApp, as required to operate the messaging service
  • Authorities when required by law

6. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, and meet legal requirements. Message logs and booking records are then deleted or anonymized.

7. Your rights and data deletion

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data. To delete WhatsApp or booking-related data held by FTS Travels, email booking@ftstravels.com with the subject “Privacy / Data Deletion” and your phone number or booking reference. We aim to respond within 48 hours.

8. Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13. Children’s travel details are provided only by a parent or guardian as part of a family booking.

9. Security

We use HTTPS, access controls, and staff permissions to protect personal data. No method of transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to prevent unauthorized access.
